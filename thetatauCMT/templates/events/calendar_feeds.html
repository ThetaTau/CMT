Calendar Feeds

Back to calendar

Subscribe to Theta Tau events from your own calendar app (Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple Calendar, …). Your custom feed links are private and unguessable — only share them with people you trust, and delete a feed to revoke access. Feeds refresh automatically and include the next several weeks of past events plus all upcoming events.

To get events that stay up to date, subscribe to a feed URL in your calendar app — don't just open the link. Opening the URL in a browser only downloads a one-time snapshot; subscribing lets your calendar re-check the URL and pick up new events automatically.

How to subscribe (Google, Apple, Outlook)
  • Google Calendar: Other calendars → + → From URL → paste the copied feed URL.
  • Apple Calendar: click Add to calendar below, or File → New Calendar Subscription… → paste the feed URL.
  • Outlook: Add calendar → Subscribe from web → paste the feed URL.
